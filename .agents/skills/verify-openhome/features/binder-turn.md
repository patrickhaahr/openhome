# Binder page turn

The household turns Binder pages on the iPad the way a real binder turns, by dragging a page from its edge or tapping the edge. This recipe extends [binder.md](./binder.md): same stack, same invented `OP99` cards, same seeded owners. Start from its **Dashboard paging** step, which gives Alex several spreads.

## Sub-features

- `binder-dash-turn` drags a page from the right edge past the midpoint and it turns; short of it, or on `pointercancel`, it springs back; from the left edge it turns back; a tap on an edge gap (in the outer pocket column, off a pocket) turns the page the same way, and does nothing before the first or after the last spread; a drag that starts on a pocket never opens its sheet, a tap still does; a drag refused at a boundary opens nothing; a press that leaves the spread ends, and the arrows still turn; with reduced motion pages swap without a turning page, in landscape and portrait.

## Steps

- **Dashboard page turn.** With Alex on several spreads (see paging), drive pointer events on `.binder-spread` with `preview_evaluate`: `pointerdown` within the outer pocket column (one sixth of the spread from its edge), `pointermove` every 16 ms toward the other side, a pause of at least 150 ms, then `pointerup`. Use `pointerId: 1`, `pointerType: "mouse"`, `button: 0`: Chrome refuses pointer capture for an id it has no pointer for. While dragging, `.binder-side[data-lifted] > .binder-leaf` rotates with the pointer (read the angle from its computed `transform`), and the page under it shows the next spread's right page. Released past the spine, it lands at -180° within about 300 ms and the folio, URL `page` and pages move to the next spread; short of it, or after a `pointercancel`, it returns to 0° and nothing changes. A drag from the left edge turns back. Dispatch a `click` on the pocket the drag started on after `pointerup`: no sheet opens. A real `preview_act` click on an edge pocket opens its sheet, and `preview_act` `drag` from a right-column pocket to the left page turns the page with no sheet. The arrows and the arrow keys animate the same turn. A real `preview_act` click on a row gutter in the outer pocket column (find one with `document.elementFromPoint` that is inside `.binder-spread` and not inside `.pocket-card`) turns the page forward on the right and back on the left. On the first spread, a backwards drag that starts and ends on the same left-column pocket, followed by a `click` on that pocket in the same task, opens no sheet. A later real click on it does. A `pointerdown` near the edge followed by `pointerout` to an element outside the spread, with no `pointerup`, leaves `Next pages` working. The preview has no reduced-motion switch: override `window.matchMedia` in the page to match `(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`, choose another owner and back to re-render, and a drag shows no lifted leaf while the release and the arrows swap pages at once. Repeat the drags after `preview_resize` to portrait: two pages stay side by side with 120×167 pockets. The preview cannot record video on a host without its browser libraries. A preview tab the user is not looking at gets no frames, so `requestAnimationFrame` and computed angles stall. Prove a settle instead with a `MutationObserver` on the Binder that records `data-lifted`, `data-settling`, the leaf's inline `transform` and `leaf.getAnimations()`: an animated turn shows a 300 ms `CSSTransition` on `transform` to `rotateY(∓180deg)` and the folio changes about 300 ms later, while a swap changes the folio with no lifted leaf.
